How it thinks

Built on the wheel, not bolted onto it

Every screen is designed around one question: what should this position do next, and why.

🎯
A ranked worklist, not a wall of tickers
Positions are scored by urgency — loss stops, assignment risk, idle capital, earnings — and surfaced in one ranked list. Tap a decision to jump straight to the position.
🛑
Two resting orders, calculated for you
Every position gets a 50% GTC take-profit and a chain-adjusted 3× eject hard stop — the exact prices to set at your broker so they work while you're not watching.
💰
Capital held to a T-bill standard
Every trade is checked against your cash-equivalent rate before you sell it, and every open position is re-checked as premium decays — so idle collateral gets flagged, not forgotten.
🔍
Put & call scanners with real liquidity checks
Ranked by yield, ATR buffer, and open interest — not premium alone. Scans three expirations so the monthly (usually the deepest market) never gets skipped.
♻️
The full cycle, not just the trade
Assignment, shares, covered calls, and being called away all roll into one running basis and one wheel P&L — so you always know what the whole cycle actually made.
📱
One tool, every device
Works from your desktop and installs to your phone's home screen. Your data follows you through your own private sync — never a shared database.

FAQ

Good to know

Is DeltaCortex financial advice?

No. DeltaCortex is a tracking and analytics tool — it surfaces numbers and flags patterns in positions you already hold, using rules you can see and adjust. It does not place trades, manage your account, or tell you what to do. Every decision, including whether to act on anything shown, is yours.

Where does my market data come from?

You connect your own market data key from a provider you sign up with directly. DeltaCortex uses it to pull quotes on your behalf — it's never stored on a DeltaCortex server, only on your device.

Can you see my positions?

No. Your positions are stored locally on your device. If you use cross-device sync, it moves through a private store that only you control — DeltaCortex has no database of user portfolios.

What strategies does it support?

Cash-secured puts and covered calls — the wheel strategy, end to end, from opening a put through assignment, share management, covered calls, and being called away.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Manage or cancel your subscription anytime from the billing link in your confirmation email — no calls, no forms.
